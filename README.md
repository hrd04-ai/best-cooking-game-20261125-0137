# 🍜 Golden Wok — Night Market Rush

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-best--cooking--game-ff6b5e)](https://make.fun/bounty/best-cooking-game-20261125-0137)

A timed night-market wok-stall cooking game. Run the busiest wok stall on the
street: chop, fry, plate and serve before the customers walk away hungry.

**Bounty:** [Make the best cooking game — best-cooking-game-20261125-0137](https://make.fun/bounty/best-cooking-game-20261125-0137)

## How to play

1. An order ticket appears on the board — it lists the dish and its ingredients.
2. Click an ingredient bowl (or press **1–6**) to send it to the chopping board.
3. Chop it with **SPACE** or by clicking the board (3 chops), then click the
   board again to toss it into the wok.
4. Watch the wok timing bar — click the wok in the **green zone** for a perfect
   cook. Too early and it goes back raw; too late and it burns.
5. Cooked ingredients land on the assembly plate. When the plate matches a
   ticket, it glows **READY TO SERVE** — click the serve window or press
   **ENTER** to serve.
6. Serve fast for tips and grow your combo multiplier. A ticket that runs out
   costs a heart; burnt food must go in the trash. Lose 3 hearts and the day
   fails.

Three days, 120 seconds each: Day 1 is gentle, Days 2–3 add combo dishes,
faster tickets and a quicker wok.

## Controls

| Key / Click | Action |
|---|---|
| Click bowl / **1–6** | Send ingredient to the chopping board |
| **SPACE** / click board | Chop (3 chops), then toss into the wok |
| Click wok | Pull food out (green zone = perfect) |
| Click serve window / **ENTER** | Serve a matching ticket |
| Click trash | Throw away burnt food |
| **P** | Pause |
| **M** | Mute / unmute |
| **ENTER** | Start / continue / restart |

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop
Chrome. No build step, no network requests, no external assets — all art is
drawn in code and all sounds are synthesized with WebAudio.
